Privacy policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Local game data

Wingtown has no player account or developer-hosted cloud save. Progress, settings, purchase entitlements, and consumed purchase transaction IDs are stored in local app storage. You can delete saves in the game or clear app data; uninstalling may also erase local data. You control any save file you manually export and share.

Advertising and consent

The Android app uses Google AdMob. The code supports rewarded and interstitial ads. It checks UMP consent status before requesting ads and shows a consent form when required. AdMob may collect and share with Google IP address (which may indicate approximate location), app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising/device identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Without consent, the code requests non-personalized ads; in some cases it requests no ads. To change your choices, use Settings → Ad privacy options in the game (or clear app data). See Google's partner data explanation and Google's privacy policy.

In-app purchases and analytics

Android in-app purchases use Google Play Billing. The app processes product IDs, purchase status, and transaction IDs to deliver or restore entitlements. The developer does not receive payment card numbers. Google's rules govern its payment and account data. There is no separate Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or other game analytics integration; AdMob's own ad measurement still applies.

Retention, deletion, and contact

Local saves remain on the device until deleted. Google's controls apply to retention and deletion of its ad and payment data. For data requests or questions, contact destek@ucenly.com. We update this policy and the Play Data safety declaration if data practices change.
